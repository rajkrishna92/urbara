The Problem

Indian farmers are losing on every front — inputs, outputs, and prices.

❌ On the Farm

  • Blind spraying: Farmers use chemicals without knowing what pest or disease is present, degrading soil and increasing costs.
  • Labour crisis: Manual transplantation and harvesting are becoming unviable due to shortage and cost of farm labour.
  • No crop planning: Decisions on crop rotation are based on habit, not data, leading to poor yields.

❌ At the Market

  • No quality traceability: Farmers cannot prove the quality of their produce, so they get low, generic prices.
  • Fragmented supply chains: Up to 30% of harvest is lost due to poor logistics and lack of direct market linkage.
  • Existing tech is shallow: Current agri-apps offer generic advice — not molecular-level, location-specific intelligence.

Our Solution

A "Phygital" Agri-OS — molecular science delivered via AI, drones, and a smartphone.

🌱 Bio-Diagnostics

Soil microbiome testing and crop disease profiling. We replace guesswork with peer-reviewed biology, identifying the exact pathogen before prescribing any treatment.

🤖 Agentic AI Advisory

Math-driven GxE (Genotype × Environment) models generate hyper-local crop rotation plans and precision spray schedules — delivered via WhatsApp and IVR in local dialects.

📊 Market Intelligence

Computer vision quality grading at the Mandi and fuzzy-logic supply chain routing help farmers prove quality and secure premium e-NAM prices.

10× better than generic advice because we act on molecular data, not assumptions.
