🏦 Non-Dilutive Grants (BIRAC BIG, NIDHI-PRAYAS, MeiTY)

At the current ideation stage, government grants are URBARA's primary source of operational funding — and critically, they carry zero equity dilution. This is the single most financially important decision for a deep-tech founding team: preserve equity at the early stage by funding pure R&D with grants, not venture capital.

  • BIRAC BIG Grant: Up to ₹50 Lakhs as non-dilutive grant-in-aid, disbursed in milestone-based installments over 18 months. Specifically designed for biotechnology-based deep-tech startups, including smart agriculture applications (Source: BIRAC, 2024). URBARA's PhD-led founding team and bio-informatic focus makes it a high-eligibility candidate. Application windows: January 1 and July 1 annually.
  • NIDHI-PRAYAS: Up to ₹15 Lakhs for prototype development at FIIT-IITD incubated companies. URBARA's incubation at FIIT-IITD provides direct access to this scheme.
  • MeiTY TIDE 2.0: ₹5–₹25 Lakhs for AI/technology startups at IIT-based incubators, covering cloud infrastructure and AI development costs.

💡 Combined Grant Potential: BIRAC BIG (₹50L) + NIDHI-PRAYAS (₹15L) + MeiTY TIDE (₹25L) = ₹90 Lakhs in non-dilutive funding available to URBARA at the ideation-to-PoC stage — covering approximately 18–24 months of R&D costs without any investor equity.

💼 High-Margin Boutique Consulting Fees (Export/Premium)

The first commercial revenue stream targets the highest-margin segment: farms and agribusinesses targeting export markets or premium domestic channels, where bio-quality certification and MRL compliance is mandatory and they can afford to pay for it.

  • Service: URBARA acts as a "Precision Agriculture Surgeon" — providing fully managed, end-to-end advisory from soil microbiome profiling through harvest quality certification.
  • Pricing: ₹15,000–₹50,000 per consultation engagement per season, depending on farm size and complexity. For FPOs managing 50+ farms, this becomes a ₹5–₹25 Lakh annual retainer.
  • Why clients pay premium: A single EU customs rejection of an export consignment costs ₹30–₹80 Lakhs in rejected produce. URBARA's MRL compliance certification reduces this risk to near-zero, making the consulting fee a low-cost insurance premium, not an expense.

📈 Success-Based Profit Sharing with FPOs

This revenue model aligns URBARA's financial success directly with the farmer's outcomes — the strongest possible trust-building mechanism. URBARA charges nothing upfront; instead, it takes a pre-agreed percentage (typically 10–20%) of the verifiable incremental yield value generated by its AI prescriptions.

  • Verification mechanism: AgriStack crop yield data + mandi price records provide objective, tamper-resistant proof of yield improvement and price realisation — enabling accurate profit-share calculation without disputes.
  • Example: If URBARA's AI helps an FPO of 200 farmers achieve ₹400/quintal better price realisation on 5,000 quintals of paddy (₹20 Lakhs incremental income), URBARA earns 15% = ₹3 Lakhs — with zero upfront cost to the FPO.

🛰️ Per-Acre / Per-Sensor Field Service Fees (RaaS)

The Robotics-as-a-Service (RaaS) model monetises the physical AI execution layer — drone spraying, soil sensing — on a per-acre or per-sensor-reading basis. This is a recurring, usage-based revenue stream that scales linearly with the number of acres served.

💻 SaaS / API Licensing for B2B Food Processors

At scale, URBARA's AI platform becomes a white-label SaaS layer licensed to large agribusinesses, food processors, and FMCG companies who want to integrate URBARA's supply-chain intelligence into their existing procurement ERPs.

  • Pricing model: ₹5–₹25 Lakhs per year per enterprise license, based on the number of FPOs connected and volume of produce traced through the platform.
  • Why FMCGs pay: FSSAI regulations and EU/UK import standards require documented farm-to-fork traceability. URBARA's API provides this as a plug-in service, avoiding the FMCG's need to build proprietary agri-data infrastructure.

🔬 Bio Quality-Testing-as-a-Service at Mandis

Setting up rapid molecular quality testing stations at major mandis unlocks a high-frequency, transactional revenue stream. Every consignment that passes through gets tested — generating per-quintal fee revenue that compounds with mandi throughput volumes.

🌾 Produce Aggregation Margin

Once URBARA has verified that a farmer's produce meets premium-grade bio-quality standards (via its own testing protocol), it acts as a digital middleman — purchasing the produce at a slight premium to the mandi spot price and routing it to FMCG/export buyers willing to pay 15–35% above mandi rates for certified, traceable supply.

📊 Market Context: India's agricultural export value was $53.1 Billion in FY2023. The premium-grade, traceable segment of this market (targeting EU/Gulf markets) commands 20–40% price premiums over standard mandi prices. URBARA's bio-certification is the gateway to this premium segment — for both domestic premium buyers and export markets.
