🤖 Cutting-edge AI-enabled Agri solutions — from field to mandi

URBARA is not another generic crop advisory app. It is a full-stack, end-to-end intelligence system that accompanies every kilogram of produce from the moment a seed enters the ground at the farm all the way until it is graded, priced, and sold at the mandi.

The system fuses three layers of AI into a single seamless pipeline:

  • Edge AI on drones & IoT sensors — capturing real-time soil moisture, leaf-reflectance (NDVI), and pest pressure at field level without requiring internet connectivity.
  • Agentic LLM orchestration in the cloud — continuously synthesizing field sensor data, satellite imagery, weather forecasts, and live e-NAM mandi prices to generate prescriptive decisions, not just descriptive insights.
  • Molecular Biology AI models — using computer-vision and bio-informatic pipelines to grade produce quality at the mandi, linking biological markers (sugar content, pathogen load) to market premiums.

📊 Market Scale: The Indian precision agriculture market is projected to reach $24 Billion by 2026 (Bain & Company). URBARA is built to capture the deep-tech, B2B2C tier of this market — the segment currently completely unserved by generic agri-apps.

🔗 Closes the Farm → Mandi supply chain loop with e-NAM & e-NWR integration

The Farm-to-Mandi supply chain in India is plagued by 5–7 layers of intermediaries, each extracting margin. A farmer selling paddy for ₹2,000/quintal may watch the same paddy sell to the end consumer for ₹5,500/quintal — a 175% markup absorbed entirely by middlemen.

URBARA's Agentic AI closes this loop by integrating natively with two government digital rails:

  • e-NAM (Electronic National Agriculture Market) — provides real-time price discovery from 1,656 mandis across 23 states, allowing the URBARA AI to recommend the optimal mandi destination based on live pricing, transport cost, and perishability window.
  • e-NWR (Electronic Negotiable Warehouse Receipts) — enables URBARA-verified, bio-quality-graded produce to be pledged as collateral for short-term credit, allowing farmers to hold grain during low-price seasons without financial distress.

💡 Impact: By eliminating 3 of 7 intermediary layers and routing via e-NAM, URBARA projects farmers can recover an additional ₹400–₹900/quintal directly into their income — a 20–45% increase in effective price realisation.
