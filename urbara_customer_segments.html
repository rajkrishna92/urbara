🌾 Smallholder Farmers — Precision Agronomy & Yield Uplift

India's 118 million smallholder farm families (owning less than 2 hectares each) are the most underserved segment in all of Indian agritech. They lack access to soil testing labs, agronomists, or reliable market information. Their average yield is 2–3x below the national potential yield — not because they are unskilled, but because they have never had access to the science available to large commercial farms.

  • What URBARA provides: WhatsApp and IVR-based advisory in local dialects (Hindi, Bengali, Odia). A farmer books a soil health check by sending a voice message. Within 48 hours, URBARA's Agentic AI delivers a personalised crop rotation plan and spraying schedule.
  • Economic impact: Precision agronomy trials across South Asian smallholder farms show 30–120% yield increases within the first two seasons of adoption.
  • CAC Strategy: URBARA does not acquire smallholders individually. They are reached at scale through FPO networks, eliminating the massive individual Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) barrier.

📊 Market Data: 80% of India's 140 million farmers are smallholders. Only 4% of smallholders currently access formal precision agronomy services. URBARA's SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market) in this segment alone is approximately 56 million farm families within 5 years of scale.

🏘️ FPOs — Collective Precision Services & Supply Chain Access

Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs) are URBARA's primary B2B distribution channel and co-delivery partner. An FPO aggregates 500–5,000 smallholder farmers into a collective purchasing and selling entity, allowing URBARA to deploy services at scale with a single contract rather than thousands of individual farmer agreements.

🏭 Small & Large Agribusinesses — Quality Grading & Logistics AI

Agribusinesses operating at the mandi and processing layer face a severe grading inconsistency problem. The same commodity grade can vary dramatically between different regions, brokers, and seasons — making procurement planning extremely unreliable.

  • URBARA's Bio Quality Grading: Uses molecular bio-markers (sugar content, moisture, pathogen load, pesticide residue) and computer vision to produce standardised, verifiable quality scores for any batch of produce — eliminating the subjective, eyeball-inspection grading that currently dominates mandis.
  • Logistics AI: Dr. Rajkrishna's Hesitant Fuzzy mathematical models calculate the optimal transportation route from farm to processing facility, factoring in perishability windows, vehicle availability, road condition uncertainty, and real-time fuel costs.
  • SaaS Licensing: Agribusinesses subscribe to URBARA's quality grading API as a B2B software service, integrating it into their existing procurement ERP systems.

🛒 FMCGs & Food Processors — Traceable, Premium-Grade Raw Supply

The food processing and FMCG sector (companies like ITC, Nestle India, Britannia, Amul, and exporters) faces extreme compliance and traceability pressure from international regulators. The EU's Maximum Residue Level (MRL) regulations and FSSAI's domestic standards require documented proof of chemical use from seed to shelf.

  • The URBARA Traceability Stack: Every farm that uses URBARA's drone spraying service generates a verified, time-stamped, GPS-tagged chemical application log. This log, combined with post-harvest molecular bio-quality testing, constitutes a farm-to-mandi chain of custody document — exactly what FMCG companies need for regulatory compliance.
  • Premium Segment Access: Produce from URBARA-managed farms that passes molecular quality certification is routed to premium B2B buyers who pay a 15–35% price premium for traceable, MRL-compliant supply.
  • Export Market: For export-grade produce (particularly for EU/Gulf markets), URBARA's bio-certification dramatically reduces the rejection risk at customs — a problem that currently costs Indian agricultural exporters approximately ₹5,000 crore annually.

🌐 Export Opportunity: India's agricultural exports were valued at $53.1 billion in FY2023. Export rejection due to MRL non-compliance affects approximately 8% of shipments by value. URBARA's farm-to-shelf traceability stack directly attacks this ₹35,000+ crore annual export leakage.
