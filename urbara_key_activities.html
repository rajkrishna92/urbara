🌱 Soil Health Check incl. Soil Microbiome Testing

Soil health testing is the foundational activity that triggers every other service URBARA provides. Without understanding the biological and chemical composition of the soil, no AI model can generate a reliable crop prescription. Traditional government soil testing labs offer basic NPK (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium) analysis, which is necessary but severely incomplete — it ignores the soil's microbial ecosystem, which determines how efficiently those nutrients are actually available to crop roots.

URBARA's approach goes significantly deeper:

  • Physical Soil Sampling: URBARA field technicians collect geo-tagged soil samples from multiple micro-plots within a single farm (accounting for spatial variability), which are then processed in an accredited lab using metagenomics protocols developed by Dr. Bidisha Bhowal at IIT Delhi.
  • Microbiome Sequencing (NGS): Next-Generation Sequencing profiles the full microbial community — bacteria, fungi, archaea — identifying beneficial organisms (like nitrogen-fixing Rhizobium strains and phosphorus-solubilizing fungi) and pathogenic organisms (like Fusarium root-rot causing species). ICAR-NBAIM's Indian Soil Microbiome Project has established reference baselines across India's agro-ecological zones that URBARA cross-references for diagnosis.
  • Biochemical Activity Assays: Soil respiration rate, enzymatic activity (urease, phosphatase), and microbial biomass carbon are measured to quantify how biologically active or degraded the soil is — translating complex biology into a simple "Soil Health Score" presented to the farmer.

🔬 Scientific Justification: Research published in Frontiers in Microbiology demonstrates that farms using microbiome-informed management practices show 15–40% reductions in synthetic fertiliser requirements with equivalent or improved yields, due to enhanced natural nutrient cycling by beneficial microbial consortia. ICAR's Indian Soil Microbiome Project is actively building the national reference database that URBARA leverages.

🔄 AI-Powered Crop Rotation Consultation

Crop rotation — the practice of growing different crops sequentially on the same land — is one of the most powerful tools for maintaining soil health, managing pests, and stabilising farm income. However, the optimal rotation is not generic; it depends on the specific soil microbiome, local weather patterns, market demand, and the genotype-specific interactions between particular crop varieties and local environmental conditions.

Dr. Rajkrishna's GxE (Genotype × Environment) interaction models are the mathematical backbone of this activity. These models, developed through his PhD research in Computational Mathematics, statistically predict how specific crop genotypes will respond to specific environmental conditions — transforming variety selection from guesswork into a mathematically optimised decision.

🤖 Agentic AI Integrating Real-Time Data for Field Decisions

URBARA's Agentic AI is the central nervous system of the platform. Unlike a simple chatbot or static advisory portal, an Agentic AI operates autonomously — it does not wait for the farmer to ask a question. It continuously monitors all connected data streams and proactively pushes alerts at the exact moment a decision window opens.

  • Real-time Data Synthesis: The agent simultaneously processes soil sensor readings (moisture, pH), live weather forecasts (IMD, Skymet), NDVI anomalies from satellite imagery, and e-NAM price movements — triangulating all inputs to determine the single most critical action the farmer should take next.
  • Prescriptive Alerts (Push, not Pull): "Your paddy field Block-A shows early leaf blight stress (NDVI drop 0.08 in 72hrs). Apply Tricyclazole 75% WP at 0.6g/L. Mandi price for Grade-A paddy is ₹2,180/quintal today (5-year high). Harvest within 9 days." — This level of specificity requires multi-domain expertise that no single agronomist can manually provide at scale.
  • Multilingual Output: All prescriptions are translated by the LLM into Hindi, Bengali, Odia, or other regional languages, delivered as voice notes via WhatsApp or IVR — eliminating literacy and language barriers.

⚙️ Technical Stack: The Agentic AI is built on an LLM orchestration layer (OpenAI GPT-4o / Gemini 1.5 Pro) with custom agricultural domain fine-tuning, connected to real-time data APIs (e-NAM, ISRO Bhuvan, IMD, IoT sensor streams) through a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) architecture.

📐 Math-Driven Logistics & Supply Chain Optimisation in Fuzzy Environments

The logistics of moving agricultural produce from farm to mandi is not a clean, deterministic problem. It involves simultaneous uncertainty across multiple dimensions: road conditions (potholed, flooded, blocked), vehicle availability (breakdown probability), weather (unexpected rain delays), and price volatility (mandi prices shift hourly). Standard logistics algorithms fail in these conditions.

Dr. Rajkrishna's research specialty — Hesitant Fuzzy Set theory applied to multi-criteria decision-making — is specifically designed for exactly this problem. It mathematically handles situations where decision-makers are hesitant or uncertain about their evaluations, and finds the optimal compromise across conflicting objectives simultaneously.

📊 Impact Justification: India loses approximately 74 million tonnes of food annually (22% of foodgrain output), with logistics inefficiency being the primary driver (NABCONS / MoFPI assessment, 2022). Even a 10% reduction in logistics losses via URBARA's math optimisation engine would represent over 7 million tonnes of food saved annually — a multi-billion rupee recovery.

🔬 Bio-Informatic & AI-Powered Disease Susceptibility and Quality Testing

This activity bridges the gap between farm-level biology and market-level commerce. URBARA uses Dr. Bidisha's molecular biology expertise to create a verifiable, science-backed quality score for agricultural produce — replacing the current subjective, eyeball-inspection grading at mandis with an objective, molecular-level certification.

  • Disease Susceptibility Screening: Before a crop is planted, URBARA screens seed varieties for genetic susceptibility to known regional pathogens (e.g., blast fungus in rice, late blight in potatoes), using bio-informatic analysis of pathogen databases maintained by ICAR-IARI. This prevents crop failure before it happens.
  • Post-Harvest Bio-Quality Testing: At the mandi, URBARA's testing pipeline measures MRL (Maximum Residue Level) compliance, sugar/starch content, moisture, and pathogen load — all of which directly determine whether produce qualifies for premium domestic markets or export.
  • Computer Vision Grading: AI-powered image analysis (trained on ICAR-certified grade standards) provides rapid visual grading (Grade A/B/C) in seconds, supplementing the molecular data with bulk-level quality estimation for the entire consignment.
  • Blockchain-Backed Certificate: Each quality test generates an immutable, timestamped digital certificate — the chain of custody document that FMCG companies require for regulatory compliance and premium payment.
