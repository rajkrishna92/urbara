🔬 R&D: Math Algorithm Optimisation & Bio-Lab Testing

URBARA's single most important cost centre is also its most strategically defensible one: the research and development of proprietary mathematical models and biological datasets that no competitor can shortcut their way into replicating. This R&D cost is explicitly designed to be covered by non-dilutive government grants, preserving equity for growth-stage scale.

  • Mathematical Algorithm Development (Dr. Rajkrishna): Development and validation of GxE yield prediction models, Hesitant Fuzzy Set supply chain optimisation algorithms, and the Agentic AI orchestration engine. These algorithms require continuous iteration against real field data.
  • Bio-Lab Testing (Dr. Bidisha): Soil microbiome NGS sequencing costs (approximately ₹3,000–₹8,000 per sample for metagenomics analysis), molecular quality testing reagents, and pathogen profiling protocol development. Volume drives unit cost down as the lab throughput increases.
  • Proof-of-Concept (PoC) Farm Trials: Controlled field trials at 3–5 pilot farms to generate validated data proving the AI prescriptions outperform conventional methods — required evidence for both grant applications and early FPO contracts.

🏦 Grant Coverage Strategy: BIRAC's Biotechnology Ignition Grant (BIG) provides up to ₹50 Lakhs as non-dilutive, milestone-based grant-in-aid for exactly this type of deep-tech, PhD-led, biotech R&D (Source: BIRAC, 2024). NIDHI-PRAYAS and MeiTY's TIDE 2.0 scheme provide an additional ₹5–₹25 Lakhs for software/AI technology proof-of-concept at IIT-incubated startups. Combined, these grants can cover the entire early R&D phase without any equity dilution.

☁️ Cloud Compute & Agentic AI Infrastructure

The cloud compute costs are URBARA's primary variable operational expense — they scale directly with usage (acres served, prescriptions generated, satellite images processed). This is the ideal cost structure: near-zero at ideation stage, growing only as revenue grows.

  • Model Training Costs: One-time GPU compute costs for training GxE and quality grading models. Google Cloud's research credits program for academic startups significantly subsidises these costs at the early stage.
  • Inference/API Call Costs: OpenAI / Gemini API costs scale per prescription generated. At ₹150–₹300 per LLM-generated prescriptive advisory, the marginal cost per farmer interaction is negligible relative to the value delivered.
  • Satellite Processing: Computer vision processing of ISRO Bhuvan imagery is compute-intensive. Cloud spot-instance pricing and batch-processing optimisation keeps this cost variable rather than fixed.

🦾 Robotic Hardware: Drones, Rovers & IoT Sensors

URBARA maintains a zero-hardware-ownership policy for its physical AI execution layer. This is the single most important cost structure decision that keeps URBARA asset-light:

  • Drone RaaS Model: URBARA coordinates with certified Garuda Aerospace / IdeaForge drone operators on a per-acre fee basis. URBARA earns margin between what the farmer pays and what the drone operator charges — without ever owning a drone. The drone operator bears all CAPEX, maintenance, and regulatory compliance costs.
  • IoT Sensor Leasing: At pilot stage, IoT soil sensors are leased rather than purchased — deploying them at FPO partner farms as "demo units" that simultaneously generate valuable training data for the AI models.

💡 Key Principle: Zero Warehouse CAPEX. Zero Drone Fleet CAPEX. Zero Cold-Storage CAPEX. URBARA owns no physical infrastructure. Every physical service is delivered through partner networks. This keeps the balance sheet clean and margins high — following the proven playbook of asset-light deep-tech platforms like Uber (no cars) and Airbnb (no hotels).

📋 Field Operations & Ground-Truth Data Collection

Field operations — physical farm visits, soil sample collection, FPO onboarding, and Kisan Mela demonstrations — are the highest-touch cost category but the most critical for building farmer trust and generating the ground-truth training data that improves model accuracy.

URBARA structures this cost as both a necessary operational expense and a data investment: every field visit generates geo-tagged, validated soil, crop, and yield data that compounds the value of the AI models over time, creating an increasing-returns data flywheel.
