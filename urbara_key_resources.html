📡 AgriStack & Satellite Imaging Data

URBARA's foundational data layer is built on India's Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) for agriculture — the AgriStack. Approved by the Union Cabinet in September 2024, AgriStack creates a unified, Aadhaar-linked digital identity for every farmer in India through a Farmer ID ("Kisan ki Pehchaan"). By integrating with this DPI, URBARA gains instant, verified access to farm boundaries, crop history, subsidy records, and landholding data — without building any data collection infrastructure ourselves.

🌱 Soil Health & Real-Time Field Data

Unlike satellite data (which captures what a farm looks like from space), soil health data reveals what is happening below the surface — the biological and chemical reality that determines actual crop outcomes. URBARA captures this through a combination of field IoT sensors and Dr. Bidisha Bhowal's proprietary soil microbiome testing protocols.

  • IoT Soil Sensors: Deployed at key field points to continuously measure soil pH, moisture, electrical conductivity, and temperature. Data streams directly into the URBARA Agentic AI engine via NB-IoT or LoRa wireless.
  • Soil Microbiome Sequencing: ICAR-NBAIM's Indian Soil Microbiome Project has catalogued microbial diversity across India's agro-ecological regions. Dr. Bidisha's research applies NGS (Next-Generation Sequencing) to profile the specific bacterial and fungal consortia that determine nitrogen fixation efficiency and disease suppression capability in specific soil types.
  • Strategic Value: URBARA's soil microbiome database — built farm by farm — becomes a proprietary, compounding dataset that no competitor can replicate without years of identical fieldwork. This is the core biological moat.

📊 Personalised Actionable Agri Insights

Raw data has no value to a farmer. URBARA's core intellectual resource is the AI pipeline that converts raw multi-modal data into a single, actionable prescription in the farmer's local language. This pipeline is built on three proprietary components:

  • GxE Mathematical Models (Dr. Rajkrishna): Genotype × Environment interaction models predict how a specific crop variety will perform in specific soil and climate conditions — enabling variety-level recommendations, not just generic crop choices.
  • Hesitant Fuzzy Set Algorithms: Applied to supply chain routing and logistics decisions where multiple conflicting criteria exist simultaneously (e.g., "minimize cost" vs. "maximize freshness" vs. "avoid road uncertainty"). These are published academic methodologies from Dr. Rajkrishna's research, now applied operationally.
  • Agentic AI Orchestration: An LLM-based agent continuously monitors all data streams and proactively pushes prescriptive alerts — not waiting for the farmer to ask, but anticipating the next critical field decision.

🤖 LLMs & Agentic AI + ☁️ Cloud Infrastructure

The AI engine running URBARA's decision layer requires world-class cloud infrastructure. Rather than building proprietary data centres, URBARA uses a managed, API-first cloud strategy that scales with revenue:

  • Google Cloud / Vertex AI: Model training, satellite data processing, and production API hosting.
  • OpenAI / Gemini APIs: Powering the Agentic AI orchestration layer — the LLM that synthesises data and generates prescriptive outputs in Hindi, Bengali, and other regional languages.
  • Microsoft FarmBeats: Edge AI integration — running lightweight inference models directly on on-farm IoT devices without requiring internet connectivity for core decisions.

🦾 Robotic Hardware + 🔬 Bio Research Labs

The physical execution arms of URBARA's AI prescriptions are agricultural robots and drones, deployed through a Robotics-as-a-Service (RaaS) model:

  • Drones (via Garuda Aerospace, IdeaForge): Research shows drone precision spraying covers 10–15 acres per hour vs. 1–2 acres per manual labourer, while reducing water use by 80–90% and chemical use by 20–50%. URBARA does not own the drone fleet; it coordinates with certified drone operators and OEMs, maintaining the asset-light model.
  • IIT Delhi & ICAR Bio-Labs: URBARA's founders have access to advanced molecular biology infrastructure at IIT Delhi for NGS sequencing, pathogen profiling, and bio-quality testing — a resource that would cost crores to build independently, but is available as part of academic incubation.
